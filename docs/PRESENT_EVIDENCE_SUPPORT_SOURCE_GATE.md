# Present-evidence support source gate

Status: canonical production boundary. This record closes the 2026-08-25
Linode pulse-support audit without defining a new observation family or
deploying a support producer.

## Result

The production command port and the final Nightshift decision boundary exist,
but no production evidence source is qualified to answer the first Linode
office's exact present-support question. The classification is:

> **SEMANTIC GAP — PRESENT SUPPORT SOURCE UNDEFINED**

`present_evidence_unavailable` means that the live cycle could not obtain an
exact `nightshift.qualified_support.v1` response from its configured
present-evidence authority. It is an operational refusal at the external
support port. It does not mean that the subject is unhealthy, that its NQ
evidence is false, or that a diagnostic acquisition should be repeated.

The missing executable is not the substantive gap. Installing a program that
merely returns `current` would turn an unresolved observation meaning into a
deployment choice. That is forbidden.

## Existing ownership and contract

Nightshift sends one sealed `nightshift.present_evidence_query.v1` to the
configured executable whose basename is exactly `pulse-support-resolver`. The
query binds the live cycle, nonce, observation, diagnostic-input set, subject,
scope, and exact delivered artifact identities. A response binds those same
coordinates, one authority identity, receiver-clock evaluation and expiry,
standing, and exact evidence or contradiction references.

The resolver port is read-only. It may consume, relate, and verify independent
support evidence. It must not:

- invoke NQ diagnostic acquisition;
- ask the diagnostic provider to run again;
- create the pulse/support occurrence used by its verdict;
- turn evidence replay into a new occurrence;
- infer present support from V3 origin or admission custody;
- emit AG, Docket, repair, restart, or other effect authority; or
- decide a stronger proposition than its qualified support source establishes.

Nightshift owns the final posture/currentness consequence. A support producer
does not emit `current = true`; a resolver does not own the resulting governed
reliance decision.

## Concrete first Linode question

The dormant observation office has one admitted NQ V3 acquisition:

```text
subject:     host:labelwatch-host
question:    nq.host.load_pressure/v1
artifact:    sha256:54c50dfca0acfaf369d7e800d585b35a26768ffefbad5741cea62c04b63bfad3
claim:       bounded condition host_load_pressure is explicitly absent
```

The claim was derived from one exact local provider report using one-minute
load and the NQ host threshold policy. Its V3 Linode origin chain establishes
where that exact acquisition originated. It does not establish that the load
claim remains true now.

Any positive production support source for this question therefore needs an
explicit, qualified law connecting its own independently acquired evidence to
that exact diagnostic proposition, subject, watcher/vantage family, and
decision policy. No such law currently exists.

## Candidate sources audited

### Nightshift operational fixture

`qualification/operational/fixtures/pulse-support-resolver` is deliberately a
deterministic process-boundary fixture. It says that it is not Pulse and uses
synthetic authority, clock, and evidence identities. It qualifies query and
response grammar only. It is not installable production support.

### Monitor/Pulse skunkworks

The separate Monitor/Pulse work has a bounded receiver-owned evaluation model,
support certificates, replay/expiry distinctions, and no actuation authority.
Its qualified Linux `/proc` producer observes aggregate cumulative CPU-idle
fraction and memory-availability fraction. It does not observe the NQ
one-minute-load proposition, has no qualified policy translating its signals
to `nq.host.load_pressure/v1`, and has no production Pulse-to-Nightshift
adapter. Its own retained contract records that Nightshift integration needs a
concrete consumer purpose and an exact freshness/custody translation first.

Reusing the Pulse name, its receiver clock, or a generic `CURRENT` category
would not supply that missing proposition-level law.

### Classic NQ liveness

The live host still emits `nq.liveness_snapshot.v1` through Classic NQ. That
record says that the Classic witness loop emitted a named generation and
contains producer-side age arithmetic. It does not bind the NQ-NG store,
watcher admission, V3 acquisition, artifact, vantage, or load-pressure claim.
The Classic Watchbill graph is retired from the canonical runtime and retained
only as historical test archaeology. Its liveness record cannot be promoted
through this resolver.

### Origin and admission evidence

The qualified Linode V3 origin proof and NQ admission provenance are exact
custody facts. Neither is a pulse and neither says that the diagnostic
proposition remains true. Treating either as present support would collapse
origin, admission, and currentness.

## Replay, staleness, and later support

The following identities remain distinct:

```text
replay(existing diagnostic acquisition)
!= deliberate successor diagnostic acquisition
!= replay(existing support occurrence)
!= produce a new support occurrence
!= resolve support applicability
```

The present contract can validate exact query/response replay and strict
receiver-clock expiry. It does not yet decide whether a later independent
support occurrence may support this earlier load observation, because the
required production support family and its applicability law are undefined.
Selecting such a law is an observation-meaning decision, not resolver
implementation.

## Deployment consequence

The Linode office remains installed and dormant. Its first cycle remains
`recovery_required` for `present_evidence_unavailable`; no canonical
observation was persisted. No pulse producer or resolver was installed, no
new diagnostic acquisition occurred, and no timer was enabled.

The next prerequisite is to ratify one production support family for an exact
Nightshift decision. That ratification must state:

1. the independently owned support proposition and producer;
2. exact subject, watcher/observation-family, vantage, source, and policy
   bindings;
3. the receiver-owned occurrence, replay, and expiry law;
4. the exact implication from the support proposition to the diagnostic
   proposition or decision being supported;
5. authentication, custody, restart, and contradiction behavior; and
6. whether later support may lawfully support an earlier diagnostic object.

Only after that contract exists may a production resolver be implemented and
deployed. Repeat diagnostic acquisition is a separate campaign and cannot be
hidden inside this prerequisite.
