## Purpose and claim boundary

SECOND-WATCH qualifies that an equivalent future Nightshift run can be launched
by one bounded operator command and thereafter use the local durable foreman—not
a long-lived outer model conversation—for ordinary scheduling and custody.

The intended claim is narrow: local DAG scheduling, provider-capacity waiting,
wake/re-evaluation, crash recovery, worker dispatch, receipt collection,
independent-lane continuation, and final closeout no longer depend on
conversational memory. It is not a claim of autonomous authority. All protected
decisions remain governed by existing Nightshift doctrine.

## Bootstrap and scheduler separation

The initiating operator action is one bounded Nightshift CLI invocation that
admits and launches the fresh packet through the actual durable foreman. After
that command returns, the initiating Codex session must not act as:

- scheduler;
- queue;
- retry or wake loop;
- state store;
- transition memory;
- provider-capacity monitor;
- receipt collector; or
- final-closeout coordinator.

Codex may participate only as a worker through the independently qualified
adapter or as a read-only auditor. Any worker occurrence is bounded by its exact
brief and adapter contract. Any audit consumes exact stores and receipts through
query-only/read-only paths.

6. another independent lane continues and reaches safe terminal custody;
7. the foreman process terminates and restarts;
8. parked state and mechanism history survive restart exactly;

## Durable wake and restart law

The parked record must retain exact refusal evidence, `wake_at`, backoff ordinal,
selected provider/model, lock policy, prior dispatch identity, and same
work-attempt identity. It must survive:

- process termination immediately after parking;
- process termination after wake eligibility but before redispatch;
- duplicate wake notifications;
- concurrent scheduler openers; and
- pathname replacement or content mutation of an untrusted store path.

One immediate transaction must decide the wake transition and create at most one
new dispatch occurrence. Alternate transition histories must refuse or converge
without duplicate provider execution. Wake time, refusal time, observation time,
receiver custody time, and foreman evaluation time remain separate.

## Independent-lane and question law

A parked lane, waiting-approval lane, or presentation-only human question must
not block a resource-independent runnable lane. Resource contention must remain
exactly attributable to closed lock identities, not a generic campaign pause.

Questions retain their exact work-item/lane identity, source finding, exhausted
evidence, safe default, consequences, and resume point. The foreman and Casework
must not answer them, synthesize approval, or grant authority.
