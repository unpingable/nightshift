# Four-office pilot 01

This document declares the first four-office governed Git pilot across
Night Shift, AG, Docket, and NQ. It is written **before** the pilot's governed
execution, and it deliberately contains no run identifiers, digests, or
verdicts — those belong to the offices that mint them, and to the private
campaign record that preserves them.

## Purpose

Demonstrate that four separately-owned offices can carry one bounded piece of
work — adding this document and one README link — from proposal to evaluated
testimony without any office absorbing another's jurisdiction:

1. **Night Shift proposes.** A bounded, advisory, read-only intention.
2. **Docket prepares.** The exact bytes of the change, as a prepared attempt.
3. **AG authorizes.** The exact Docket-prepared attempt, burning its own
   one-use decision authority.
4. **Docket executes and settles.** A broker-authored Git effect, with
   evidence.
5. **NQ evaluates.** What the recorded testimony does and does not establish.
6. **Night Shift consumes.** NQ's receipt, emitting only a read-only
   disposition.

A human remains the only party able to decide what happens after that.

## Jurisdiction

- **Night Shift** proposes bounded intent. It does not authorize, execute,
  retry, repair, clear findings, resolve contradictions, or emit any execution
  capability. Its proposal binds intent only — never exact content, and never
  authority.
- **AG** authorizes an exact prepared attempt presented to it. It does not
  execute, and its issuance establishes nothing about repository state.
- **Docket** prepares exact bytes and executes exactly what was authorized. It
  does not decide epistemic admissibility, and its settlement is not a safety
  claim.
- **NQ** evaluates evidence against registered claims and configured consumer
  profiles. It emits testimony and refusals, never actions.

## What this document establishes — and does not

- The presence of this document establishes only that the pilot was declared
  and that the governed change carrying it was executed through the offices
  above.
- It does **not** establish `safe_to_merge`, review-readiness, or any other
  claim about this repository. Those claims live with NQ's registry and remain
  subject to its refusal vocabulary.
- No claim is made here that the pilot has already succeeded. The pilot's
  actual receipts, identifiers, and outcomes — including any refusals or
  indeterminacies — are preserved in the private campaign record, which is the
  only place they may be read.
